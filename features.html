Features

Everything you need to go from an idea to a ready-to-post short video.

Script library

Write your own short tips or generate them with an AI assistant. Each script follows a clear format: hook, three points, call to follow.

Natural voice-over

A text-to-speech voice reads your script in French, with timing for every word.

Animated template

Animated background, numbered cards that appear as each point is spoken, and a progress bar.

Automatic subtitles

Large, readable subtitles synchronized with the voice, built for phone screens.

Royalty-free footage

Use your own folder of royalty-free background clips, or keep the built-in animated background.

Send to TikTok drafts

One click or one command uploads the finished MP4 to the drafts of your connected TikTok account.

Local-first

The tool runs on your own computer. Your videos and your access token stay on your machine.

Simple to run

A small desktop web page lets you connect your account and upload a video in a few clicks.
