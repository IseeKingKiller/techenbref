Frequently asked questions

Does TechEnBref post on TikTok automatically?

No. It uploads the video to your drafts. You review it and publish it yourself in the TikTok app.

Does it copy videos from other creators?

No. Videos are created from scripts, a synthetic voice and royalty-free or animated visuals. The tool is meant for original content only.

What data does TechEnBref access?

Your display name and avatar (to show which account is connected) and the permission to upload videos to your drafts. See the Privacy Policy.

Where is my access token stored?

Only on your own computer. TechEnBref does not run a server that collects your data.

How do I disconnect my account?

Click Disconnect in the tool, or revoke access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and services permissions.

Are videos labeled as AI-generated?

Yes. Videos that use synthetic voices or AI-assisted scripts should be labeled as AI-generated content on TikTok.

Is TechEnBref affiliated with TikTok?

No. It is an independent tool that uses TikTok's official developer APIs.
