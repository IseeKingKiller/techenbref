Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data we access

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (display name and avatar) so that it can upload videos to your drafts on your behalf. It only uses the permissions you approve.

2. How data is used and stored

The access token is stored locally on your own computer. It is used only to send the videos you choose to your TikTok drafts. We do not operate a server that collects your data, and we do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties.

3. Videos and content

Videos are generated and stored on your own computer. Script text may be sent to an AI provider to be written, and royalty-free footage may be downloaded from stock sites. No TikTok user data is sent to these services.

4. Revoking access and deletion

You can revoke the tool's access at any time in the TikTok app (Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and services permissions). You can delete all locally stored data by deleting the tool's folder on your computer.
