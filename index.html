Short AI & tech tips videos, made simple.

TechEnBref is a desktop tool for creators. It helps you turn a tip into a clean, animated vertical video, then sends it to your own TikTok drafts so you can review and publish it yourself.

How it worksSee features

Why TechEnBref

A consistent look

Every video uses the same animated template, colors and subtitles, so your channel feels familiar from one video to the next.

Original content only

Videos are built from your own scripts, a synthetic voice-over and royalty-free footage. TechEnBref does not copy or repost other creators' videos.

You stay in control

The tool uploads videos to your TikTok drafts. Nothing is published until you review the draft and post it in the TikTok app.

Works with TikTok

TechEnBref connects to your account with TikTok Login Kit and uses the TikTok Content Posting API to upload videos to your drafts. You can disconnect it at any time from the tool or from your TikTok settings.
