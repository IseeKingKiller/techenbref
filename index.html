TechEnBref

TechEnBref is a personal desktop tool that prepares original short videos about AI and tech tips and uploads them to the creator's own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The creator connects their own TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit.
  2. The tool generates an original video (script, voice-over, subtitles, royalty-free background footage).
  3. The creator reviews the video and the upload is sent to their TikTok account through the Content Posting API.

The tool does not copy, scrape or repost videos made by other creators.
