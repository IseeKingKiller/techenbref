How it works

  1. Install the tool
    TechEnBref runs on your computer (Windows) and opens a small page in your browser at localhost.
  2. Connect your TikTok account
    Click Connect TikTok. You log in on TikTok's own page and approve the permissions. TechEnBref never sees your password.
  3. Create a video
    Pick a script. The tool generates the voice-over and the animated video as an MP4 file on your computer.
  4. Send it to your drafts
    Choose the video and click Send to TikTok drafts. The video is uploaded to your TikTok inbox as a draft.
  5. Review and publish in TikTok
    Open the TikTok app, check the draft, add your caption and publish it yourself.
